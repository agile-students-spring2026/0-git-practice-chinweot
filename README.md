## Selected Article: Computer Science in Agricultural Innovation

[Applications of Computer Science in Agricultural Engineering — Krzysztof Górnicki](https://www.mdpi.com/2076-3417/13/10/6071)

**Author:** Krzysztof Górnicki
**Publication Date:** May 15, 2023

## Thoughts

This article stood out to me because it showed how computer science is being used to improve agricultural engineering through modeling, artificial intelligence, and machine learning. 

One concept I found interesting was the use of AI to detect plant diseases and optimize resource usage such as water and energy. These applications show how technology can help address issues in food production and sustainability.

Overall, this article helped me recognize the growing importance of interdisciplinary collaboration. As technology continues to evolve, engineers must be prepared to apply their skills across diverse fields to solve complex problems that affect humanity.
